Zodimath Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Zodimath is made by Troy Lee.

Zodimath is a maths game for children. We collect as little as possible, we never show ads, and we never sell or share personal information for advertising.

Playing without an account

Children can play without any account. Progress (stars, stickers, streak and the chosen birth year and buddy) is stored only on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

Parent accounts (optional, shared with Zodiwords)

A parent can create an account to back up progress and use several devices. The same account works in our other children's app, Zodiwords: a parent signs in once, and the child profiles they add appear in both apps. Children never sign in and never give us an email address.

WhatWhy
Parent's email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) or a sign-in from Google or AppleTo sign in and to recover the account
Each child's nickname, birth year and chosen zodiac buddyTo show the right profile, the right class and the buddy
Each child's Zodimath progress: stars per topic, stickers, streak, practice-test scores and questions to reviewTo back it up and restore it on another device

With "Sign in with Apple", Apple may give us a private relay address instead of the real email.

Reading questions aloud

Zodimath reads questions and cheers aloud. It uses the device's own text-to-speech and a few short recordings bundled in the app. It does not use the microphone and never records voices.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, and no voice recordings.
  • No chat or messaging between users.

Service providers

Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in. Google and Apple handle their sign-in when a parent chooses them. The app's few voice recordings were made in advance with Microsoft Azure AI Speech; no user data is sent to Microsoft.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while the account exists. In the app's parent area (Settings, behind the grown-ups check) a parent can remove a child, which deletes that child's profile and backups, or delete the whole account, which deletes the account and all its children and backups straight away, in both Zodimath and Zodiwords. Progress kept only on a device is removed by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

Zodimath is designed for children under 13 and follows the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Only a parent can create an account or add a child. Settings, account features and outside links sit behind a grown-ups check. Parents can delete their child's information at any time in the app, or by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents in the app.
