Zodimath support

Questions, ideas or a problem? Email troylee.zodimath@gmail.com and we will reply within a few days.

Common questions

Does my child need an account?

No. Children play straight away. A parent can optionally sign in (Settings, behind the grown-ups check, then Parent account & children) to back up each child's progress. The same parent account works in Zodiwords.

How does the app choose what to practise?

From the birth year chosen at the start, the app picks the level that fits your child's age. Any other level can be picked at the top of the home screen, and nothing is locked.

Can I switch the language?

Yes: Settings, behind the grown-ups check, then Language.

I cannot hear the questions read aloud.

Questions are read by the device's own voice. Check the volume and the silent switch, and that a voice for the language is installed (iOS Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices).
