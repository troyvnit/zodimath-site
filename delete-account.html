Delete a Zodimath account

A parent can delete their account at any time. The account is shared with Zodiwords, so deleting it removes it, every child profile and all backups in both apps. Playing never needs an account.

In the app

  1. Open Zodimath and tap the gear on the home screen.
  2. Answer the grown-ups check, then tap Parent account & children.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes the account straight away. To remove only one child, tap the pencil next to the child and choose Remove.

Without the app

Email troylee.zodimath@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Zodimath account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The parent account and sign-in details.
  • Every child profile (nickname, birth year, buddy) and all progress backups, in Zodimath and Zodiwords.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Progress stored only on a device stays there until the app is deleted.
